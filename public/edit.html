Chain of Command

People are arranged by level (top = Level 1). Everyone on a level reports to the level above — no per-person “reports to”. Click a person to edit their details. Drag people between levels. On desktop, drag the ⋮⋮ handle to reorder levels. On mobile, use ▲ ▼. People are auto-sorted by rank within each level. On mobile, tap a person to edit in a full-screen sheet.

Backup

Live: data/data.json. One in-app backup: data/data.backup.json. One external backup: sqndash-data-backup/ (next to the app). Changes autosave after 30s idle. Download includes branding (loading logo) and chain of command; large logos make the JSON bigger.

Loading logo

Best format: PNG with a transparent background (square works best). JPG will show a solid box on the cream loading screen. SVG is accepted too. Images are resized on upload and replace any previous logo (the old one is discarded).
