General

How long after boot the device powers itself off. Takes effect from the next boot onwards, not immediately.

Automatic switches to the compact small-screen layout on screens 800 pixels wide or less, or 500 pixels tall or less (like the official 7-inch Pi display and smaller). Use the other two options to force one or the other.

The small-screen layout always uses the built-in weather display, so a weather widget is only used on larger screens.

Carousel widgets

Tick the widgets you want in the rotating panel. Anything unticked isn't shown, and each ticked widget gets 10 seconds on screen.

News is skipped while this is empty. Paste any compatible embed (e.g. FeedGrabbr) to change the feed.

The Instagram widget is skipped while this is empty.

Extra embed widgets

Add as many as you like - a weather radar, a countdown, a map, another social feed. Paste the embed code from the service, choose a display name (only shown on this page, to tell them apart) and the title that appears above it on the screen. They rotate after the built-in widgets, in the order listed here. Get free embed snippets from services like elfsight.com or sociablekit.com; they refresh on the screen automatically every 10 minutes.
